# Roadmap

## M0: Decisions

- [ ] Launch region
- [ ] Capture app: native or Flutter
- [ ] Alignment spike: 50 phone photos of one square through COLMAP/GLOMAP,
      with and without sensor priors; measure time and registration rate
- [ ] Name check: trademark, domain, app stores

## M1: Capture and upload

- [ ] Capture app recording GPS, heading, pitch, roll, focal length
- [ ] Web upload with EXIF
- [ ] Blurring of faces and plates at upload

## M2: Alignment

- [ ] Candidate pairs from position and heading
- [ ] Batch structure-from-motion per cluster
- [ ] Georeferencing and per-photo pose accuracy

## M3: Map and walk-through (MVP)

- [ ] Map with pins and explorable places
- [ ] Walk-through viewer with direction arrows and smooth transitions
- [ ] Reporting and licence choice

## M4: Growth

- [ ] Fill-the-gaps guidance in the capture app
- [ ] Incremental registration of new photos

## M5: v2

- [ ] Time slider and then-and-now
- [ ] Community: profiles, likes, comments, groups
